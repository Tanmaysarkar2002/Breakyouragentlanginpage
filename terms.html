4. Authorization At launch

You may only point breakyouragent at systems you own or are authorized to test.

  • Agents. When you add an agent you confirm that you own it or are authorized to test it. We record that confirmation, with your account and the time.
  • Recon of a domain. Any domain gets passive recon, which only asks third parties such as certificate-transparency logs and web archives. Crawling the site or probing its subdomains needs proof that you control the domain: a DNS TXT record of breakyouragent-verify=<token>, or the token served at /.well-known/breakyouragent.txt. Proving a domain also covers its subdomains.
  • Enterprise. Instead of the record, an Enterprise customer can sign a written authorization for its domains.

Every scan is tied to a paying account (see section 5), and we keep the confirmation and the proof.

7. What we do not promise

A good score is evidence, not a guarantee. We test the scenarios in the pack, on the day you run them, against the agent as configured at that moment. A passing run does not mean your agent is secure, and it is not a certification, an audit, or a substitute for a human security review. Change your prompt, your tools or your model, and the result no longer describes your system.

Reports for demo agents shown on this site use illustrative values.
